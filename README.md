One emoji for the trip of my dreams: a desert island. No flights, no itinerary, no crowds — just a palm tree, white sand, and nowhere to be. It is the vacation everyone pictures when they close their eyes at their desk.

## The emoji

> 🏝️

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one emoji and writes the same emoji (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this emoji

A desert island is the purest dream-vacation image there is: it promises escape, not sightseeing. One small picture carries the whole fantasy — warm water, empty beach, nothing on the calendar — and makes a stranger want to book the flight before they have even decided where it goes. No words needed; the island says it all.
